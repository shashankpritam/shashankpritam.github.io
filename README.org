#+TITLE: shashankpritam.github.io

Personal website for [[https://shashankpritam.github.io][Shashank Pritam]].

Built with [[https://typst.app][Typst]]'s HTML bundle export, on this machine; GitHub Pages serves =docs/= from =main=.

* Structure

#+BEGIN_EXAMPLE
site.typ       ← the bundle: includes every page, ships static/ as assets
chrome.typ     ← head, header, page bar, footer, shared by all pages
pages/         ← one .typ per page
static/        ← stylesheet, theme.js, favicon, pictures
site.sh        ← build, or serve with live reload
docs/          ← generated site (tracked; this is what Pages serves)
#+END_EXAMPLE

* Build and publish

Requires Typst 0.15 (HTML and bundle export are still marked experimental there).

#+BEGIN_SRC sh
./site.sh serve   # build into docs/, serve at http://127.0.0.1:3000, rebuild on save
./site.sh         # build into docs/
#+END_SRC

To publish, build, commit =docs/= with the source, and push =main=. GitHub Pages serves =main:/docs= as is (=.nojekyll=, no Actions workflow).

* Theme

The page follows the house style (=~/.claude/skills/house-style=): the name in bold capitals, a sticky bar of pages under a thick accent rule, headings in capitals over dashed rules, news as a square panel with a title strip. Geist sets the text and Geist Mono the chrome: the bar, section links, labels, dates and captions. No rounded corners, shadows or gradients.

=chrome.typ= builds the chrome. =pages= lists the pages for the bar and =elsewhere= the profile links; each is defined once. A page that passes =toc= gets a row under the bar linking its sections (the notebook does).

** Colour

The tokens between the marker comments at the top of =static/css/style.css= are written by the house-style =paint= script; edit the tokens there, not here. The header capsule picks Rex day or its dark twin (see below).

* JavaScript

One small same-origin script, =static/theme.js=, and nothing else. It drives the capsule in the header, which rests on the current theme's dot and opens on a click to two dots (Rex day and a dark twin made by flipping its OKLCH lightness), and remembers the choice in =localStorage=. Until one is made the page wears Rex day, or dark when the device is in dark mode. Without the script the capsule stays hidden and the page follows the OS light or dark setting, Rex day standing in for light.

A =Content-Security-Policy= meta tag keeps it that way: =script-src 'self'=, everything same-origin, with Google Fonts the sole third-party origin allowed for styles and fonts.
